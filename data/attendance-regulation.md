## Section 4: Attendance Requirements

### 4.1 General Requirement

All students are required to maintain a minimum attendance of **75%** in all courses across the academic session. Attendance is measured as a percentage of classes held.

### 4.2 Definition of Attendance

Attendance is calculated as:
- Total number of classes attended / Total number of classes held × 100

Classes are held according to the academic timetable. If a student is present for any part of a class session, they are marked present for that session.

### 4.3 Recording and Monitoring

- Attendance is recorded using biometric systems or manual registers.
- Attendance reports are generated weekly and made available to students.
- Students below 70% are issued a warning letter.
- Students below 60% are placed on academic probation.

---

## Section 5: Medical Exemptions and Condonation

### 5.1 Medical Leave

Students may request condonation of absence due to medical reasons:
- Medical certificate must be issued by a recognized hospital or medical practitioner.
- Certificate must state the period of illness and recommended rest.
- Application must be submitted within **7 days** of return to campus.
- Condonation is approved if the medical condition is substantiated and relevant.

### 5.2 Condonation Conditions

Condonation of up to **5%** of the required attendance may be granted for:
1. Documented medical emergencies
2. Hospitalization (with medical certificate)
3. Death of a close relative (with death certificate)

Students granted condonation are required to maintain a minimum of **70%** attendance in the remaining sessions.

### 5.3 Non-Condonable Absences

The following absences cannot be condoned:
- Unauthorized leave
- Absences due to personal/family matters (except death)
- Repeated unexcused absences

## Section 6: Examination Eligibility

### 6.1 Eligibility Criteria

A student is eligible to write the examination if they meet **both** of the following conditions:

1. **Attendance Threshold:** Attendance of at least 75%, OR attendance of at least 70% with condonation approval
2. **Academic Standing:** No outstanding disciplinary action or academic probation

### 6.2 Exemption from Attendance

Students in the following categories may be exempted from the 75% requirement:
- Students with disability (as recognized by the disability office)
- Students with approved medical conditions requiring modified participation
- Students undertaking approved field work or internship

Exempted students must maintain a minimum of **60%** attendance.

### 6.3 Late Examination Permission

Students who fail to meet attendance requirements by the examination deadline may appeal to the Dean of Academics. Appeals must include:
- Documented evidence (medical, family emergency, etc.)
- A letter from the course instructor supporting the appeal
- A personal statement explaining the circumstances

---

## Section 7: Processes and Appeals

### 7.1 Condonation Application Process

1. Student submits condonation application with supporting documents
2. Course coordinator reviews the application
3. Dean of Academics approves or rejects
4. Student is notified within 5 working days

### 7.2 Appeal Process

Appeals against examination ineligibility must be filed within **3 days** of notification:
- Appeal must be submitted to the Dean of Academics
- Appeal can be based on medical grounds, compassionate circumstances, or procedural error
- Dean's decision is final

### 7.3 Documentation Requirements

All documentation must be:
- Original or notarized copies
- Legible and complete
- Submitted within specified timelines
- In English or officially translated

---

## Section 8: Effective Date

This regulation is effective from the academic session starting September 2026 and supersedes all previous attendance regulations.
